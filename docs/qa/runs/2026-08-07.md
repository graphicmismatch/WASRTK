## Findings

### Paste does not preserve background before commit

**Item:** Selection Safety — "Create rectangle, lasso, polygon, and
magic-wand selections; copy, cut, paste, move, and cancel each."

**Symptom:** Pasting a clipboard selection wrote the pasted pixels directly
onto the layer before the paste was committed. Dragging the pasted
selection and then canceling (Escape) restored the *already-baked* pasted
pixels instead of the true original background underneath — the opposite
of what commit/cancel does for a detached (cut/moved) selection, where the
pre-move layer state is snapshotted and restored correctly.

**Status: fixed.** Resolved in commit `70846b9`
(`fix: paste preserves layer pixels under detached selection`), Phase 1.8
of the WASRTK simplification effort. The fix makes `pasteSelectionFromClipboard`
capture the pre-paste layer state the same way `detachSelectionFromLayer`
already did, so commit/cancel restore the correct pixels in both cases.
The fix is covered going forward by the `paste` probe in
`tests/smoke/renderer-smoke.js` (drag a pasted selection, cancel, and
assert the original background pixel is restored byte-for-byte; then
paste + commit and assert the clipboard content did land) and by a
permanent item in the manual checklist (see
`docs/qa/manual-interaction-checklist.md`, Selection Safety section).
